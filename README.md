HomeStay SL – Project Summary

1. Background and Problem

HomeStay SL is a proposed student accommodation website designed for Limkokwing University of Creative Technology Sierra Leone. 
Many students travel from different parts of Sierra Leone to study in Freetown and face difficulties finding safe,
affordable, and reliable accommodation near campus. Currently, students depend on Facebook groups, word-of-mouth,
and physically searching for rooms. These methods can expose them to scams, unsafe housing, high rental prices, and
unreliable information.

2. Proposed Solution

HomeStay SL aims to solve these problems by providing a centralized online platform where verified landlords can 
advertise available rooms and apartments. Students will be able to search for accommodation based on their budget, 
preferred location, room type, and available amenities. The website will also include property photos, descriptions, 
contact information, and an interactive map showing accommodation locations in relation to the university.

3. Target Users

The main users of HomeStay SL are new students relocating to Freetown, continuing students seeking accommodation closer to 
campus, international students who need reliable housing information, and landlords who want to
connect with student tenants.

4. User Research and Personas

The project identifies three user personas. Fatmata Sesay represents a first-year student from Kenema
who needs safe and affordable accommodation. Ibrahim Kamara represents a local student who wants to reduce 
commuting time by moving closer to campus. Mohamed Bangura represents a landlord who needs a reliable platform to
advertise vacant rooms and find suitable student tenants. These personas help guide the website’s design to meet users’ 
needs.

5. Website Structure and Navigation

The website will contain four main sections: Home, About Us, Browse Homestays, and Contact Us. 
The homepage will introduce the platform and display featured accommodation. The About Us page will explain the
platform’s purpose and benefits. The Browse Homestays section will allow students to search and filter properties, 
while the Contact Us page will provide contact forms and relevant contact information.

6. User Journey

The student journey begins on the homepage, where users view featured accommodation. They then browse listings, 
apply search filters, review property details, and contact landlords. After confirming availability and arranging the 
necessary details, students can proceed with booking and follow up with the landlord when necessary.

7. Website Wireframes

The project includes low-fidelity wireframes created in Figma to illustrate the proposed website layout.
These designs include the landing page, About Us page, accommodation search page, advanced filtering page, and Contact Us
page. They serve as visual guides for developing the final website.

8. Importance of the Project

HomeStay SL is intended to improve student welfare by making accommodation searches safer, easier,
and more organized. It will help students compare available housing options while providing landlords with a 
dedicated platform to reach prospective tenants. The project also proposes a role for the university in verifying 
landlords and supporting safer off-campus accommodation.

Conclusion

HomeStay SL is a proposed web-based accommodation platform that connects Limkokwing University Sierra Leone students 
with verified landlords. Through searchable listings, property details, filters, and an interactive campus-proximity map,
the project aims to simplify the process of finding accommodation in Freetown. The research, user personas, website 
structure, user journey, and wireframes provide a foundation for developing the final website as part of the DCOMP 204
Web Design assignment.
