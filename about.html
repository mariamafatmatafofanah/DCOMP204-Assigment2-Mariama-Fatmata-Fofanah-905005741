A trusted accommodation verification network dedicated to Limkokwing University Sierra Leone students.

01 / SYSTEM SCOPE & MISSION

Mission Statement & Off-Campus Standards

We connect Limkokwing students with safe, verified and comfortable accommodation options. Every landlord is checked, every listing is verified, and all housing information is transparent and easy to access.

Our student-first standards ensure that you get safe, affordable and quality housing — so you can focus on your studies and future.
